Could we design with AI and skip the pixel-perfect step?

The problem

The usual process had seven steps: the BA gathers and writes requirements, the designer draws wireframes, the BA checks, the designer builds a hi-fi prototype, then developers start.

Evidence: log, photo, quote or old screen

What I found

Most of the waiting sat in two places: getting from requirements to something people could react to, and getting from an approved design to code.

Flow diagram with the breaking point marked

The decision

Option A

Keep the classic process: wireframes by hand, then a pixel-perfect prototype in Figma.

Why not: slow at both ends.

Option B

Why not

Option C · chosen

AI drafts, people decide: AI makes the wireframe, and the hi-fi is built directly in HTML.

Why this one: developers get code.

The solution

Screen with numbered notes

1 Wireframe by AI

AI turns the UI requirements into a first wireframe.

2 Hi-fi in HTML

The polished version is built as a working page, not a mockup.

3 Handoff as code

Developers receive code, or it goes straight to test.
